Who we are

UR Properties is a real estate brand based in Puttur, Dakshina Kannada (D.K.), Karnataka, India.

We focus on trust, design quality, transparency, and customer-first service. We specialise in:

  • Layout / plot development
  • Construction
  • Residential projects (apartments, villas, and plotted developments)

We have 10+ years of experience in real estate and emphasise integrity, innovation, and sustainable practices.
